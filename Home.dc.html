Frequently Asked

What couples ask us.

When should we reach out to you?+

Ideally twelve to eighteen months before the date. Because we take on only a limited number of celebrations each year, couples often reach out at the moment of the engagement — though we consider every enquiry, whatever the timeline.

Do you take weddings outside France?+

Yes. We design and produce celebrations across France and internationally, surrounding ourselves with a curated local team of exception, sourced and directed by us.

How many celebrations do you take each year?+

A deliberately small number — a handful of projects a year. This allows us to remain deeply involved in every story, from the first conversation to the final guest.

Can you work with our own venue and vendors?+

Gladly. If you already have a venue or vendors dear to you, we fold them into the creative direction. If not, we compose the entire team around your vision.

How is your fee structured?+

Our fee is bespoke to each project and reflects its scale, location and complexity. We share a detailed proposal after our first conversation, with full transparency.

What does the first conversation involve?+

An unhurried, no-obligation moment — by video or at the atelier in Paris. We listen to your story and how you picture the day, then tell you honestly how we might compose it together.
